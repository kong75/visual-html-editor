# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Address concrete impact while allowing room for misunderstanding.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

During private development, collaborators may report concerns to the repository owner through their existing private collaboration channel. Before inviting a public community, the owner must publish a monitored enforcement contact and an alternate contact for concerns involving a maintainer. Those identities are intentionally pending in the [public release checklist](./docs/public-release-checklist.md).

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request a behavior change, or restrict participation. They should consider severity and recurrence, explain their decision privately where possible, and protect reporter privacy. A report involving a decision-maker should be handled by an uninvolved maintainer or the designated alternate.
